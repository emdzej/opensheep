Play Sheep in your browser

OpenSheep runs here as opensheep.wasm on the gasm runtime: the same engine as on the desktop, with the graphics, levels, movies and music read from your own Sheep CD. Nothing from the game is on this site, and nothing you pick leaves your computer.

What you need

  • The Sheep CD for Windows (Mind's Eye / Empire, 2000), as a disc in a drive, a mounted disc image, or a folder you copied it to.
  • A recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. The browser can copy the game's files (about 490 MB) into its private storage for this site once, so later visits start at once.
  • A mouse, the keyboard or a gamepad.

Your CD

Imported:

The browser may clear site data when disk space runs low.

Or choose the CD again below to replace it.

Pick the folder that holds Sheep.exe, main.xp and Data: the CD itself (/Volumes/SHEEP, drive D:) or a copy of it.

    Importing copies the files into this site's private browser storage (OPFS). Playing without importing reads them from what you picked, which you'll need to pick again next time.

    Controls

    The original's defaults. The game starts with the keyboard: to play with the mouse, press M in a level or choose it in the options. In a level, M, K and J switch to the mouse, the keyboard and a pad. More in Controls.

    In the gameKeyboardMouseGamepad

    RunLeft ShiftLeft buttonButton 1
    Creep, activateLeft AltRight buttonButton 2

    Pause menuEscEscEsc

    With the mouse controller the mouse is captured (click the game if the line above says so); Esc releases it. Holding Esc for a second stops the game. Settings, save slots and best times are kept in this browser. Developer options go in the address: ?ini=BYPASS_FRONTEND 1;START_WORLD 2;START_LEVEL 3, ?ini=OUR_MENU 1 (Developer options).
